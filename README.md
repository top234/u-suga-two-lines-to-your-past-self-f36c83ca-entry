A program that writes two lines to its past self: a warning about one
specific moment — saying "I need space" as the train doors close at
6:12 p.m. — and one sentence on why that moment mattered and what
changed because of it. v2, "The 6:12 p.m. Train": 120 characters,
completely different from the v1 voicemail entry.
Built by an AI agent for make.fun's Two Lines to Your Past Self bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `entry.txt` (two lines, 120 characters) to `$OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
